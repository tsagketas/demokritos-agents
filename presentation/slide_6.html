Ροή πειραμάτων και αρχιτεκτονική

Matrix games (MP, RPS)

  1. agent1.act(game), agent2.act(game) ταυτόχρονα
  2. payoff1=get_payoff(a1,a2), payoff2=−payoff1
  3. agent1.update(a1,payoff1,a2), agent2.update(a2,payoff2,a1)
  4. get_strategy(), distance_to_nash, exploitability, αποθήκευση

Turn-based (Grid)

  1. current_player → act(game,state)
  2. game.step(action) → (reward,done,next_player)
  3. update(...,next_state,done)
  4. done→reset, καταγραφή rewards

Αρχιτεκτονική

games/: BaseGame, MatchingPennies, RPS, GridGame. get_payoff, best_response, get_nash.

agents/: FP, QLearning, StochasticQLearning, Minimax. act, update, get_strategy, reset.

experiments/: fp_vs_fp, fp_vs_rl, rl_vs_rl. ExperimentRunner.

analysis/: metrics.py, visualizer. distance_to_nash, exploitability, external_regret.
